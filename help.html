Booking flow

Search for a movie, open details, choose a theatre and showtime, select seats, fill checkout details, pay via demo payment, and view your ticket in My Bookings.

  • Search by movie title, actor, genre, language, theatre, or location.
  • Use the AI seat recommendation for the best available seats.
  • Coupons: CINE10, MOVIE50, FIRSTSHOW
  • Voice commands such as “Show action movies”, “Search Leo”, and “Open theatres” work with CineBot.
